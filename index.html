Sobre el Apartamento

About the Apartment

Apartamento moderno de 1 dormitorio ubicado en el corazón de Downtown Miami, en el edificio de la calle NE 6th St. Ofrece vistas espectaculares a la ciudad, acceso a piscina y gimnasio disponible 24 horas. Su ubicación céntrica permite llegar caminando a Bayside Marketplace, Bayfront Park y a varias estaciones del Metromover.

Modern 1-bedroom apartment located in the heart of Downtown Miami, in the building on NE 6th St. It offers spectacular city views, pool access and a 24-hour gym. Its central location allows walking to Bayside Marketplace, Bayfront Park and several Metromover stations.

Desde: Apartamento (159 NE 6th St, #3313, Miami, FL 33132)
Hasta: JP Morgan (80 SW 8th St, Unit 100 - Mary Brickell Village)

From: Apartment (159 NE 6th St, #3313, Miami, FL 33132)
To: JP Morgan (80 SW 8th St, Unit 100 - Mary Brickell Village)

PASO 1 STEP 1

Caminar unos 2-3 minutos hacia el SUR (1 manzana) hasta College North Station (100 NE 5th St).

Walk about 2-3 minutes SOUTH (1 block) to College North Station (100 NE 5th St).

Sal del edificio en NE 6th St y baja una cuadra al sur hasta NE 5th St. La estación está elevada, junto a NE 1st Ave.

Exit the building on NE 6th St and walk one block south to NE 5th St. The station is elevated, next to NE 1st Ave.

PASO 2 STEP 2

Subir al Metromover: BRICKELL LOOP (Sentido sur).

Board the Metromover: BRICKELL LOOP (Southbound).

El servicio es gratuito. Busca el tren con dirección a Brickell / Financial District.

Service is free. Look for the train heading towards Brickell / Financial District.
